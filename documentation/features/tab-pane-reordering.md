# Tab and Pane Reordering

## What

Tabs in the tab bar can be dragged left or right to change their order. Panes within a tab can be dragged to change their position in the grid.

## How

**Tabs:** Click and hold any tab button and drag it horizontally to the desired position. A highlighted border indicates the drop target. Release to drop.

**Panes:** Click and hold any pane and drag it to another pane's position within the same tab. A highlighted border indicates the drop target. Release to drop.

Both operations persist across app relaunches.

## Scope

Pane reordering is within a single tab only. Moving a pane to a different tab is not supported.
